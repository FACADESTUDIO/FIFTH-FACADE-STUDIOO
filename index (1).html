Fifth Facade Studio is a design-led architectural consultancy that reimagines the roof as the most expressive layer of built form. The studio focuses on creating climate-responsive, human-centered environments where geometry, material, and social interaction converge.

Rooted in contextual sensitivity, the practice engages with architecture as both a spatial necessity and a cultural experience — where form, shadow, and airflow tell the story the other four facades cannot.

Design Approach 04 / 06

At the
Intersection

Architecture at Fifth Facade Studio emerges at the intersection of Climate, Context, and Concept. By elevating the roof to its rightful status as the "Fifth Facade," we manipulate volume, shadow, and airflow to fundamentally transform the user's experience of built space.

Every project seeks a precise balance between functional rigor and poetic expression — where the pragmatic and the beautiful are not opposites, but inseparable partners.

"Rooted in contextual sensitivity, the practice engages with architecture as both spatial necessity and cultural experience."

Vision Statement

To establish a global benchmark for concept-driven architecture that proves sustainable, climate-responsive design is inseparable from bold architectural identity.

Fifth Facade Studio is a specialized architectural practice dedicated to experiential design that is deeply rooted in material honesty, cultural context, and the transformative power of the roof.

The Studio 05 / 06
Yash
Chandwani

Principal Architect & Founder · Fifth Facade Studio

Fifth Facade Studio was founded in Ahmedabad in 2025 with a singular conviction: that architecture's most transformative canvas is the one most often overlooked — the roof.

The studio operates at the intersection of design research and built practice, developing a body of work that challenges conventional thinking about how buildings meet the sky. Each project is an experiment in making the "fifth facade" the most memorable element of the design.

Based in Ahmedabad — a city with a rich tradition of thoughtful craftsmanship and climatic ingenuity — the practice draws deeply from its context while reaching toward a global conversation about experiential architecture.
